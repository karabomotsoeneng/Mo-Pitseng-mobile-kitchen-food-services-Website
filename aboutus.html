About Us

Our Story

Mo'Pitseng is a family-run mobile kitchen started by Thabiso Motsoeneng in November 2023 in Phola Park, Bochabela. The name means "in the pot", a nod to traditional slow cooking over firewood.

It all began with potjiekos dinners for friends and family at home. That joy grew into a business serving traditional home-style plates alongside kasi favourites like dibeko (vetkoek), skhumbane and chips with every side.

We keep our prices affordable so everyone, from working families to learners at the local schools, can enjoy the taste of home away from home.

Our Mission

To provide delicious and affordable food while creating a welcoming environment for our customers.

Our Vision

To grow Mo'Pitseng into a recognised South African food brand while celebrating local food and culture.

Our Values

  • Quality Food
  • Affordable Prices
  • Good Customer Service
  • Community
  • South African Heritage
  • Passion for Food

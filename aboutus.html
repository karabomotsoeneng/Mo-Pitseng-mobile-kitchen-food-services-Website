About Mo'Pitseng Mobile kitchen services

The discovery of Mo'Pitseng mobile kitchen services

Mo'Pitseng Mobile Kitcehn Food Services was bought and started in the year 2023 of november. It is a family buisness created by the owner Thabiso Motseneng in desprate need for his own industry of businesses to help his children strive in it.Mo'Pitseng is a traditional saying "in the pot" meaning traditional slow-cooking methods over firewood. We also strive in fast foods like bunnychows,vetkoeks(dibeko) and chips served with ingrediants that are of your own comfort. He started this business out of the love of cooking traditionally where it started by inviting a group of friends at his home for potjikoes(small pot food) to the mobile kitchen and serving people the love of his own passion. This business is his pride and joy and a name to his own name.

Purpose

To serve people with food that will remind them of home and to give services to the children that come from school hungry for food that will make them full and happy.
